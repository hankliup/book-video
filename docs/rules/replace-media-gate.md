The boundary sweep proves only "the frame shows what the caption track says at that moment" — it uses the track as its own truth, so an axis that drifts sixteen seconds still scores 156/156; the two axis gates that came before it compare *axis to axis*, so an axis whose rows are each a couple of seconds off its own sentence still passes them. Only the per-row coverage gate sees 《恶女的告白》 2026-09-28: 88 rows filling 227.6 s, six gates green, and 40 rows whose caption was not on screen while its words were spoken (|Δstart| median 3.04 s, max 11.82 s). The sweep has its own failure mode, the **near tie**: it scores every candidate caption by IoU and takes the argmax, so two rows that differ by one glyph — 「她握了七十二秒」 vs 「她哭了七十二秒」 — differ by a handful of pixels, and a 0.001 IoU gap turned that into a false red (《三体3·死神永生》 2026-10-03, 2 of 310 points, verified by cropping the actual frames). When the losing margin is under `NEAR_TIE`, `scripts/qc-caption-alignment.py` now votes on the *difference region* instead of on the ratio: it counts frame ink only inside the pixels where the two candidates disagree, overturns the argmax only when the expected row carries strictly more ink there, calls an exact tie clean, and prints the counter-evidence in the note when it still reports a mismatch. Non-vacuity is checked at the frames where the twin really is on screen (vote −22 → still MISMATCH). The sweep samples **frames, not seconds**: `scripts/qc-caption-alignment.py` quantizes each sample point to the film's frame grid (`round(t*fps)`), derives the body time *back from that frame index*, and seeks half a frame early, while `check-episode-delivery.py` feeds it the body's true start as `成片 nb_frames − body-bg nb_frames` through `--pre-body-frames`. Seeking with a rounded decimal is not equivalent — at 30fps `n/fps` is a repeating decimal for two of every three indices, `f"{t:.4f}"` rounds it **up**, and ffmpeg returns the first frame whose pts is ≥ the seek, so the instrument was reading the neighbour frame; and the seconds form of that offset is `round(hook_voiceover, 2) + INTRO_TRIM` while the assembly concatenates frames (《阿特拉斯耸耸肩》 2026-10-07: 215 + 90 = 305 frames = 10.1667 s, seconds said 10.18 s). Half a frame of coordinate error is exactly enough to walk an `end−0.06s` sample into the 1–2 frame blank that legitimately separates two rows across a breath, and ten points printed 「画面上没有字幕 · 带内零墨迹」 on a film whose ink was verified frame by frame (body 1636–1701 all carry row 19, 1702–1703 are the blank, 1704 starts row 20). Neither `--min-ink`, nor `NEAR_TIE`, nor the ±1-frame 「边界空帧」 exemption moved — the fix is *which frame gets asked*, asserted both ways by `scripts/tests/test-caption-frame-sampling.py` (healthy fixture green point by point; the old decimal seek genuinely landing in the blank on the same clip; a track written 3 frames longer than the pixels still red, so the gate keeps its teeth; a sample before the body start refused outright instead of reported as 「该时刻无字幕」), registered in `check.mjs` with static guards on `seek_time` / `round(t * fps)` / `--pre-body-frames` / `ffprobe_frames`. The water gate is mandatory because a missing ripple is the one defect that passes every other check: duration, captions, loudness, and A/V alignment all stay green while the cut has sound but no visible water. Never substitute a single-frame eyeball check for it, and never patch a subtitled final with `scripts/add-water-ripple.py` — re-render the body background. The hook video must also be fps-normalized **before** the seconds trim (`fps={FPS},trim=0:{hook_dur}` in `hook_vf`): the hook renders at 15fps, and trimming on seconds first quantizes it up to the 1/15 s grid, putting the body up to 2 frames later than the audio timeline — the recut 《乞力马扎罗的雪》 2026-10-07 measured a water audio/visual gap of +2.05 frames against the ±2.0 tolerance; trimming on the 30fps delivery grid brought it to +0.05. A changed hook voiceover length shifts `PRE_BODY`, so re-check the water gate after every hook re-record. Re-render the intro whenever `images-body/result-bridge.png` changes, because the intro result page is a copy of that file and the seam gate otherwise reports a misleading "wrong episode intro". The fallback assembles from `tmp/preview-<slug>/` — compute the slug with the same `episode-slug` algorithm (`node -e` with `slugifyEpisodeName`), never guess it from a `tmp/` listing: preview dirs are hashed, concurrent sessions own sibling `preview-*` dirs for other episodes, and handing the assembler another episode's `PREVIEW` splices that episode's hook+intro into the film. The fallback's intro-ownership check (`intro/media/pages/result.png` must be byte-identical to this episode's `images-body/result-bridge.png`) is what catches it — it redded 《乞力马扎罗的雪》 2026-10-07 before a wrong-episode film could activate, after a session passed a sibling dir found by `ls | grep` instead of the computed slug.
